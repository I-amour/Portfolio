side-hustle-tax-toolkit SHIPPED · LIVE · PAID

Millions of UK sellers on Vinted, eBay and Etsy don't know if they owe HMRC anything — so I built the answer. A free browser-based tax checker (vanilla JS, no frameworks, no data leaves your device) that computes 2026/27 Self Assessment liability in real time, paired with a formula-driven bookkeeping spreadsheet sold as a paid product. Designed, built, launched and marketed end-to-end by me: the maths, the ledger-book UI, the hosting, the store, the community launch.
